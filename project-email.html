Email Triage & Routing Bot

n8n Workflow & AI Email Automation

 

Overview

An automated inbox management system built in n8n that categorizes client emails and generates context-aware draft replies to maintain communication outside of office hours.

The Problem

High volumes of inbound client emails pile up unanswered, causing critical delays in response times for urgent requests and manual fatigue for routine inquiries.

The Solution

A continuous n8n workflow monitoring the inbox, filtering out spam, evaluating intent using Google Gemini AI, and either preparing an automated draft or sending an instant Slack alert for urgent issues.

Build Process

  • Step 1: Inbound Webhook
    Gmail node triggers on new incoming unread emails in the designated mailbox.
  • Step 2: AI Categorization
    Gemini AI evaluates the email content to categorize it as Inquiry, Urgent, or Routine.
  • Step 3: Intent Routing
    A Switch node processes the email based on the AI's determined category.
  • Step 4: Action & Escalation
    Urgent emails trigger a Slack notification to the team. Routine emails prompt a Gmail node to create a contextual draft reply.
