AWS Data Pipeline & Business Intelligence Dashboard

View Repository

Overview

Developed a unified Business Intelligence dashboard using AWS and Amazon QuickSight. It aggregates raw data from CRM deals, marketing campaigns, and support tickets to provide clear visibility into the marketing funnel, sales pipeline, and customer health.

The Problem & Solution

Problem: Scattered financial and marketing metrics across multiple platforms make it difficult to spot real-time revenue trends, calculate conversion rates accurately, and allocate budgets effectively.

Solution: Engineered a centralized data pipeline that merges disparate datasets into a single source of truth, using a left-join strategy anchored on the CRM table to preserve every closed deal, even without a matching marketing or support record.

Build Process

  • Step 1: Data Integration
    Joined raw CSV datasets (Deals, Campaigns, Tickets) on a shared account ID using left joins, then resolved the resulting row multiplication so downstream revenue metrics stayed accurate instead of inflated.
  • Step 2: Data Cleaning
    Standardized data types and added calculated fields, days-to-close, campaign ROI, and ticket resolution time, to support cross-referencing and deeper analysis.
  • Step 3: Visualization
    Built a three-sheet interactive dashboard tracking total leads, response rates, conversions, win rates, and account health, with filters, one-click filtering, and cross-sheet navigation.
  • Step 4: AI-Powered Q&A
    Configured a Quick Chat Topic with business-context instructions and synonyms, so stakeholders can ask natural language questions and get answers grounded in the correct dataset.
