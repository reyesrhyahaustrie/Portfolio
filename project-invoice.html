Invoice & Data Entry Automation

Automated pipeline via Google Sheets, intercept data via Gemini AI, and generates invoices.

The Problem

Manual transcription of session details into invoices introduces human error in dates, client names, and pricing logic.

The Solution

Engineered an automated pipeline that intercepts unstructured Google Sheet rows, utilizes Gemini AI to sanitize and validate formatting, and outputs pristine invoice data. Anomalies are automatically routed to a manual review queue.

Build Process

  • Step 1: Trigger
    New row added to Google Sheets.
  • Step 2: Processing
    Gemini AI node parses and cleans the data.
  • Step 3: Logic
    If/Else node checks for anomalies.
  • Step 4: Output
    Generates a finalized PDF invoice or flags for review.
