IT Helpdesk & Staff Asset Tracker

Centralized monday.com workspace featuring an IT ticketing system and a BYOD hardware/access tracker with custom board automations.

The Problem

Managing IT support requests, employee SaaS access, and BYOD hardware audits via email threads or static sheets leads to missed requests, security vulnerabilities during offboarding, and disorganized asset tracking.

The Solution

Built a robust IT Operations workspace in monday.com consisting of two integrated boards. It utilizes custom forms for ticket submission and native board automations to streamline status updates, SLA tracking, and staff offboarding.

Build Process

  • Step 1: Ticketing System
    Configured a board with an external form for staff to easily submit IT bugs, password resets, and provisioning requests.
  • Step 2: Asset Management
    Built a tracking board to monitor BYOD device types, minimum internet speed requirements, and SaaS access states.
  • Step 3: Custom Automations
    Set up trigger (e.g., automatically moving a row to the "Offboarded" group when access is revoked).
  • Step 4: Status Monitoring
    Applied color-coded priority columns and resolution date tracking to maintain IT operations SLAs.
